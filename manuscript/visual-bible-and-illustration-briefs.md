# Little Fart Face Learns About Banking

## Visual Bible and Illustration Briefs — v1

## Art direction

- Vintage 1950s American children's picture book: gouache, ink outlines, warm aged paper, cheerful flat colors.
- Mischievous educational satire, never mean or frightening.
- Portrait pages assembled as 16 wide two-page spreads. Keep faces, important objects, and all text away from the center gutter.
- FF2K orange, visor blue, warm cream, muted teal, mustard, brick red, and charcoal form the core palette.
- Embedded words should be rare. Typography and explanatory diagrams should be added during layout, not trusted to image generation.
- Banking scenes feel orderly, fluffy, and politely absurd. Bitcoin scenes become more open, geometric, and globally connected.

## Character sheets

### Little Fart Face

- Child-sized FF2K mascot, approximately 9 years old in proportion and body language.
- Orange Bitcoin gas mask with two circular filters, each bearing a clean Bitcoin symbol; bright blue visor.
- White ribbed tank top, navy shorts, orange-and-cream sneakers, small teal tool backpack.
- Expressive through posture, hands, visor tilt, and mask angle; the mask never exposes a human face.
- Curious, confident, mechanically gifted, occasionally too literal.
- Required turnaround poses: front, three-quarter, profile, back; pointing, thinking, fixing, surprised, proud.

### Banker Buck

- Friendly middle-aged buck deer, tan fur, small antlers, round spectacles, teal vest, cream shirt, mustard bow tie.
- Neat but approachable. Never sinister. His explanations should feel candid.
- Carries a ledger tablet and old-fashioned fountain pen.
- Required expressions: welcoming, explaining, cautious, amused, concerned.

### Node

- Inventive 10-year-old girl with dark curly hair, round blue glasses, mustard hoodie, teal overalls, red high-top sneakers.
- Travels on a homemade solar-powered skateboard with visible but simple wires and a tiny antenna.
- Carries a small single-board computer and uses diagrams rather than jargon.
- Required poses: riding, kneeling beside a device, teaching, checking a rule, warning about keys.

### Bea Baker

- Cheerful black bear, brick-red baker's cap, cream apron, rolled sleeves, flour on one cheek.
- Starts with a tiny battered oven and later stands beside a large productive oven.
- Practical entrepreneur, not helpless borrower.

### Porkfolio

- Small round ceramic piggy bank, pale pink with orange patches and one blue coin-slot stripe.
- Silent visual commentator. Eyebrows and tiny feet allow reactions.
- Required reactions: hopeful, alarmed, skeptical, relieved, trying to eat a paper satoshi.

## Environment anchors

### Fart Face Fix-It Stand

Wooden curbside workbench beneath a striped teal-and-cream awning. Pegboard tools, hand-painted repair sign, three recurring objects: red wagon, blue scooter, yellow mechanical duck.

### First Fluffy Bank

Small neighborhood bank with rounded stone columns, teal carpet, warm wood counter, brass lamps, and soft cloud motifs. It should look trustworthy and slightly over-padded.

### Bitcoin visual language

Use a shared public notebook, transparent linked wagons, repeated independent computers, orange verification checks, and a world map. Avoid glowing magic coins, cyberpunk clichés, rockets, price charts, or “crypto” imagery.

## Cover

Little Fart Face proudly hands one gold-colored dollar coin across a bank counter while a politely ridiculous bookkeeping machine behind Banker Buck produces a tall stack of loans and IOU slips. Porkfolio looks alarmed. Large clear title and “BY FF2K.” Existing cover establishes the visual target.

## Spread briefs

### Spread 1 — The Fix-It Stand

Wide neighborhood scene. Little Fart Face repairs the red wagon while the blue scooter and mechanical duck wait in line. Three happy customers pay him; ten dollars accumulate in a jar. Porkfolio waits open-mouthed at the bottom corner. Leave upper-left sky quiet for text.

### Spread 2 — First Fluffy Bank

Exterior-to-interior cutaway of the cozy bank. Little Fart Face stands on tiptoe at Banker Buck's counter holding ten dollars. A thought bubble-like inset shows his imagined private vault drawer with the bills being fluffed by tiny feather dusters. Keep the inset wordless; label added in layout.

### Spread 3 — The Bank's Promise

Banker Buck turns a monitor toward Little Fart Face. Show the physical bills joining ordinary bank cash while the monitor displays a simple account balance. Little Fart Face begins his three-question notebook. Use visual arrows, but add explanatory labels later.

### Spread 4 — Bea Needs an Oven

Bea rolls a tiny smoking oven into the bank on a squeaky cart and presents a sensible bakery plan. Little Fart Face peers toward the vault, expecting Buck to retrieve ten personal money boxes. Buck instead reviews Bea's papers.

### Spread 5 — New Bank Money

Clean, playful balance-sheet scene. Banker Buck enters the approved loan; Bea's account gains a bank balance while a matching loan card appears on the bank's side. Little Fart Face watches the paired entries balance on a large brass scale. Exact numbers and labels added in layout.

### Spread 6 — Not an Endless Muffin Machine

Fantasy gag: Little Fart Face imagines Banker Buck pressing a button marked with an infinity symbol while money and muffins bury the town. The real Buck gently switches the imaginary machine off. Around them, four sturdy gates symbolize good borrowers, capital, liquid funds, and rules.

### Spread 7 — The Ten-Coin Picture

Little Fart Face holds an old dusty textbook showing ten coin shapes entering and nine leaving. Banker Buck rolls down a modern diagram with several different constraints surrounding the bank. Porkfolio wears tiny reading glasses and looks offended by the outdated page.

### Spread 8 — When Promises Work

Bea's bakery now bustles around the large oven. Customers buy muffins; Bea sets aside a repayment envelope. A small inset shows settlement moving between two different banks. Little Fart Face enjoys a muffin while realizing credit helped create something productive.

### Spread 9 — The Payment That Took a Vacation

Sunday at the Fix-It Stand. The repaired mechanical duck is packed for an overseas customer. A comical chain of closed bank doors, paperwork desks, clocks, and fee booths blocks the payment path. Node enters dynamically on her solar skateboard from the right.

### Spread 10 — A Rulebook Without a Boss

Node and Little Fart Face sit beneath the awning with several small independent computers arranged in a circle. Each displays the same simple rulebook and matching check mark. No central throne or boss computer exists. Their notebook questions appear as layout typography above them.

### Spread 11 — The Shared Ledger

A visual metaphor fills the spread: transparent wagons linked in sequence, each carrying sealed transaction envelopes. Many independent node characters inspect the newest wagon against identical rulebooks. One noisy rogue machine offers a malformed wagon; everyone rejects it.

### Spread 12 — Twenty-One Million

Node uses twenty-one large orange tokens as a visual counting lesson, then divides one token into a shower of tiny paper satoshis under a magnifier. A fixed measuring track ends firmly at 21 million. Porkfolio attempts to eat one paper sat and instantly regrets it.

### Spread 13 — The Key

Node demonstrates a wallet as a key keeper. The bitcoin remains represented in the shared ledger in the background; a secret key authorizes a payment without moving a physical coin out of the wallet. Split the spread between confident self-custody and a cautionary lost-key shadow. Never depict or print a real seed phrase.

### Spread 14 — No Magic Money

Four honest tradeoff vignettes: a price seesaw, a scammer in an obviously fake orange-coin costume, a one-way payment arrow, and Little Fart Face carefully protecting a backup with an adult. Node points to a plain sign: different trust, different risks, more responsibility—added in layout.

### Spread 15 — Three Useful Tools

Little Fart Face organizes three clearly distinct tools on his workbench: cash in Porkfolio, a bank card/account for ordinary payments, and a small hardware wallet practice setup supervised by an adult. A silly fantasy volcano contains an untouched sign reading “BAD IDEAS”; Banker Buck safely stands nowhere near it.

### Spread 16 — Better Questions

Return to the lively Fix-It Stand. Bea brings muffins, Node carries her skateboard, and Banker Buck arrives wearing the uncontrollably spinning mechanical tie. Three customers offer cash, bank payment, and bitcoin. Little Fart Face consults his question notebook. End on warmth, competence, and curiosity.

## Back matter illustrations

- A simple visual glossary using the recurring objects and characters.
- A parent-and-child checklist with the three money questions.
- A small “find Porkfolio” activity pointing back to his reaction on each spread.

## Continuity checklist

- Two Bitcoin-symbol filters and one blue visor on Little Fart Face in every appearance.
- Same tank top, shorts, shoes, and backpack unless a scene explicitly removes the backpack.
- Never reveal Little Fart Face's face.
- Mechanical duck is yellow; wagon red; scooter blue.
- Node's glasses blue, hoodie mustard, overalls teal.
- Bank palette is teal/cream/brass; Bitcoin diagrams orange/blue/charcoal.
- Porkfolio appears somewhere on every spread.
- No real private key, seed phrase, QR code, account number, or investment claim.
