# Feedback Guide

Thanks for reviewing the draft.

## Useful feedback

- Identify the exact page or spread.
- Quote the short line or describe the illustration in question.
- Explain what confused, bored, amused, or bothered you.
- If suggesting a factual correction, link to a primary source when possible.
- If reporting a child's reaction, include the child's approximate age but no identifying information.

## Please avoid

- Investment advice or price predictions.
- Generic cryptocurrency promotion; this book is specifically about Bitcoin.
- Uploading private keys, seed phrases, account numbers, or personal financial information.
- Reposting manuscript pages or artwork elsewhere without permission.
