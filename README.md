# Little Fart Face Learns About Banking

An illustrated children's-book draft by FF2K about bank money, loans, trust, and Bitcoin.

The story follows Little Fart Face from his neighborhood Fix-It Stand to First Fluffy Bank, where he learns that a bank balance is a promise from the bank and that new bank deposits can be created when banks make loans. He then meets Node and learns how Bitcoin uses a publicly checkable rule set, a shared transaction history, and holder-controlled keys.

The recurring questions are:

1. Whose promise is it?
2. Who makes the rules?
3. Can I check for myself?

## Draft status

This is a working review edition, not a finished commercial release. The banking and Bitcoin explanations are intentionally simplified for ages 7–10, but the manuscript avoids the outdated claim that a modern U.S. bank merely keeps 10% of each deposit and lends the other 90%.
